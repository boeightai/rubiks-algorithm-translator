# WIRED Beginner Method: Algorithm Reference

Source video: **How to Solve a Rubik's Cube | WIRED** (WIRED, 24:12, uploaded 2019-09-05)
URL: https://youtu.be/R-R0KrXvWbc
Companion article: https://www.wired.com/story/how-to-solve-a-rubiks-cube-step-by-step/

Purpose: durable reference for the Way of the Cube tutorial, which is a companion to the WIRED solve strategy. Algorithms are listed in the order the video teaches them. Timestamps are from the video.

## Steps 1–2: Daisy and white cross

No algorithms. Step 1 makes the daisy (white edges around the yellow center). Step 2 matches each daisy petal to its side center and turns that face 180° to form the white cross.

## Triggers (taught 7:23 / 7:42, used in Steps 3–4)

1. **Right Trigger**: `R U R'` (7:23)
2. **Left Trigger**: `L' U' L` (7:42)

## Step 3: Solve the bottom layer (8:10)

No new algorithm. Use the triggers:
- White sticker on a top-layer side: line the adjacent side color up diagonally with its center, then do the left or right trigger.
- White sticker on the bottom layer facing out: do one trigger to bring it up, then solve as above.
- White sticker on the top face: park it over a non-white bottom sticker, do the same trigger twice, then solve as a normal side-facing corner.

## Step 4: Solve the second layer (12:20)

3. **Insert edge to the right**: `U R U R'` (U + Right Trigger), then fix the displaced first-layer corner with the matching trigger as in Step 3 (~13:03)
4. **Insert edge to the left**: `U' L' U' L` (U' + Left Trigger), then fix the displaced corner the same way (~13:21)

If a middle edge is wrong but every top-layer edge has yellow, pop the bad edge out with a trigger, then insert it normally.

## Step 5: Yellow cross

5. **"fururf"**: `F U R U' R' F'` (16:32)
   Cases: no yellow edges, a line (hold it vertical), or a backwards L (edges at 12 and 9 o'clock). Same algorithm each time.

## Step 6: Solve the yellow face (17:35)

6. **Sune**: `R U R' U R U2 R'` (18:05)
   0 or 2 yellow corners: put a yellow sticker in the upper-right of the left face. 1 yellow corner ("fish"): point the fish's mouth down and to the left. Repeat as needed.

## Step 7: Position the corners (19:10)

7. **Corner positioning**: `L' U R U' L U R' R U R' U R U2 R'` (19:33)
   The 8th move cancels the 7th (`R'` then `R`), as the video notes; the second half is the Step 6 algorithm. If one face already has matching corners, hold that face in your left hand. If none match, do it once, then again as needed. Then turn U so the corners line up with their centers.

## Step 8: Position the edges (21:46)

8. **Cycle edges clockwise**: `F2 U R' L F2 L' R U F2` (22:22). Hold the solved side away from you.
9. **Cycle edges counterclockwise**: `F2 U' R' L F2 L' R U' F2` (22:44)
   If no side is solved, do one cycle, point the new solved side away, and do it again.

## Notes on sources

- Triggers are spoken in words in the video, not written on screen; notation here is converted from the spoken description and matches the companion article.
- Step 5 in this video is `F U R U' R' F'`, not the more common `F R U R' U' F'`.
- The companion article writes the left-hand "white on top" case as `L' U L L' U L`; the video says to do the left trigger (`L' U' L`) twice, so the article's unprimed `U` looks like a typo.
- Auto-captions mishear "fururf" and "F2"; on-screen notation is treated as authoritative.
